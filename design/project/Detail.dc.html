About Online Ruler

Online Ruler turns your screen into a measuring tool that matches real-world sizes. Every display packs its pixels at a different density, so a plain on-screen ruler is rarely accurate — a centimeter on a laptop isn’t a centimeter on a phone. Online Ruler fixes that with a quick calibration that works out your screen’s pixels per inch (PPI), then draws its ticks at true physical size.

You can calibrate three ways: pick your device from a list, enter your screen’s diagonal size, or hold a standard bank card against the screen and drag until the outline matches. Once calibrated, measure in centimeters, inches or pixels, use any of the four ruler edges, and line things up with crosshair guides.

What you can do

  • Measure in cm, inches or pixels
  • Calibrate by device, diagonal or bank card
  • Use four adjustable edges
  • Align with crosshair guides

How to use it

  1. Open Online Ruler and choose a calibration method.
  2. Match the on-screen guide to your device, screen size or a bank card.
  3. Hold the object against the screen and read the measurement.
  4. Switch units or drag a different ruler edge as needed.
